Terms & Conditions

Please read our rental terms carefully before booking a self-drive vehicle with us.

1. Driver Eligibility & License

The renter or primary driver must hold a valid driving license (active for a minimum of 2–3 years). Additional authorized drivers must be registered prior to the vehicle handover. Unauthorized driving is strictly prohibited.

2. Payments & Security Deposit

Full rental charges and refundable security deposits must be paid in advance. The deposit is held against any potential traffic fines, toll fees, minor damages, or missing vehicle accessories.

3. Fuel & Vehicle Condition

Vehicles are handed over with a specific fuel level and must be returned in the exact same condition. The hirer is fully responsible for bearing all fuel costs and routine fluid checks throughout the rental period.

4. Prohibited Uses

Vehicles strictly cannot be used for sub-renting, motor sports, speed racing, towing, illegal activities, or transporting hazardous goods. The vehicle must remain within permitted geographical limits unless prior approval is given.

5. Maintenance & Responsibilities

The hirer is responsible for taking good care of the vehicle during the rental period, including daily checks (engine oil, water/coolant, and tire pressure). Damages caused by gross negligence or dry running will be charged accordingly.

6. Extensions & Cancellations

Any request to extend the rental duration must be informed and approved at least 24 to 48 hours before the agreement expires. Cancellation charges may apply based on the timing of the notice relative to the pickup time.

7. Vehicle Handover & Delivery

Vehicles must be collected from and returned to our designated office or branch unless prior arrangements are made. If doorstep delivery or drop-off is requested, additional service charges will apply based on the travel distance.
