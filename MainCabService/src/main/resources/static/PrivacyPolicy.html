Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to Aura Cabs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our self-drive vehicle rental services in Sri Lanka.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a vehicle booking, register as a driver, or contact us, we may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, driving license details, and rental dates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process vehicle bookings, verify driving eligibility, communicate with you, manage security deposits, and ensure a smooth rental experience.

3. Data Protection & Security

Aura Cabs takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies to improve functionality and user experience. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services (such as email or hosting providers) to operate our website and communicate with customers. These providers are obligated to keep your information secure.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Delivery & Location Data

If you request doorstep vehicle delivery or pickup services, we collect and store your specific location addresses and route details strictly for the purpose of facilitating the transport and managing service charges safely.
